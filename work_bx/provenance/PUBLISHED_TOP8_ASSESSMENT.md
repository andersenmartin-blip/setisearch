# Prior independent assessment of the Ma candidate set

Primary sources: [Ma et al., Nature Astronomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.12670); [Margot et al., AJ 166, 206 (2023), section IV.3 and Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.02712v4).

Margot and colleagues recovered MLc3, MLc4, MLc5, MLc7 and MLc8 and interpreted them as terrestrial interference. OFF-source detections supported four cases; MLc5 also relied on its similarity to MLc4 and their approximately 4 MHz spacing. They did not attempt MLc1, MLc2 or MLc6 within their chosen drift range. Thus this does not establish an eight-of-eight rejection.

Their comparison corrects drift signs and observation epochs in the earlier table. It also distinguishes snippet-center frequencies from actual signal frequencies. Any future local audit must use stored observation headers, test both drift signs and check projected tracks against the available crop.

These are published external findings. BX has not obtained or numerically reanalyzed the TOP8 pickle, and makes no independent confirmation claim.
