# SETIsearch — Reproducerbar arkivbaseret teknosignaturforskning

*English summary: Open, prospectively locked archival technosignature research by Martin Andersen (MSc Astrophysics, University of Copenhagen), spanning nearly five spectral octaves (`0.0905–2.515 µm`, `4.796` octaves) across space Lyman-limit/FUV/NUV (`FUSE`, `HST/COS G130M`, `HST/STIS E140M` & `E230M/E230H`), ground-based near-UV (`UVES_NUV`), optical (`HARPS`, `HARPS-N`, `ESPRESSO HR/UHR`, `OHP SOPHIE`, `OHP ELODIE`, `CFHT ESPaDOnS`), far-red (`UVES_RED`, `CARMENES_VIS`), near-infrared YJH (`NIRPS_HE`), thermal $K$-band (`CRIRES+`), and simultaneous YJHK (`CFHT SPIRou`) echelle laser & frequency-comb spectroscopy across both hemispheres and space (alongside `FEROS`), multi-epoch planetary-rest-frame `1.4 GHz` narrowband radio searches (`seti-repeater`, Parkes/GBT), and TESS specular light-sail photometry. Across all tracks — culminating in the completed **250-star (`1,759`-exposure, `569.36 h`, 14-spectrograph / 16-arm) Unbroken Space-UV & All-Sky Panchromatic `D079–D089` Survey** with **100% (`23/23`) Parkes/GBT 1.4 GHz radio-echelle closure** (`24` direct overlaps) and **uniform all-sky `COMB_V4_1` execution across 100% of `D079–D089` (`284` stacks / `1,759` exposures)** ([`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf) · [`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md) · [`paper/main.tex`](paper/main.tex)), reaching **0.0973 W (`97.3 mW`)** (`HST_STIS Proxima Cen`), **0.2194 W** (`HST_COS GJ 832`), **0.4294 W** (`FUSE Alpha Cen A`), **0.80 W** (`UVES_NUV Proxima Cen`), **0.5181 kW** (`SOPHIE GJ 625`), and **9.03 W** optically on Proxima Centauri, and a **1.7494 kW** median across **121 `< 10 pc` stars** — **0 verified technosignature detections** are claimed; every negative branch, `G4_V40` FUSE dual-channel LiF/SiC coincidence (`8/8` pass, `8/8` artifact veto) & Fiber-B regression (`577 -> 0`), uniform all-sky `COMB_V4_1` (`5,434 -> 15 -> 0 ETI`), forensic closure (`127` post-`G6` in `D089` + `15` all-sky `COMB_V4_1`), and pure-stdlib numerical audit (`16/16 PASS`) is preserved.*
