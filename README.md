# SETIsearch — Reproducerbar arkivbaseret teknosignaturforskning

*English summary: Open, prospectively locked archival technosignature research by Martin Andersen (MSc Astrophysics, University of Copenhagen), spanning more than four spectral octaves (`0.1145–2.515 µm`, `4.457` octaves) across space far-UV (`HST_STIS E140M`), ground-based near-UV (`UVES_NUV`), optical (`HARPS`, `HARPS-N`, `ESPRESSO HR/UHR`), far-red (`UVES_RED`, `CARMENES_VIS`), near-infrared YJH (`NIRPS_HE`), thermal $K$-band (`CRIRES+`), and simultaneous YJHK (`CFHT SPIRou`) echelle laser & frequency-comb spectroscopy across both hemispheres and space (alongside `FEROS`), multi-epoch planetary-rest-frame `1.4 GHz` narrowband radio searches (`seti-repeater`, Parkes/GBT), and TESS specular light-sail photometry. Across all tracks — culminating in the completed **200-star (`1,391`-exposure, `335.26 h`, 9-spectrograph / 10-arm) All-Sky Panchromatic `D079–D087` Survey** and **206-System Multi-Messenger Synthesis** ([`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf) · [`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md) · [`paper/main.tex`](paper/main.tex)), reaching **0.0973 W (`97.3 mW`)** in space far-UV (`1275 Å` `HST_STIS`), **0.80 W** in the near-UV (`3180 Å` `UVES_NUV`), and **9.03 W** optically on Proxima Centauri (now quadri-regime Space-FUV+NUV+Opt+NIR across `0.1275–1.6642 µm`), **0.7105 W** on `GJ 754`, **2.155 W** on `AD Leo`, **2.50 W** (`ESPRESSO`) on `PM J11413`, **13.69 W** on `GJ 1061`, **73.90 W** (`705 nm`) and **730.5 W** (`1310 nm`) on `DX Cnc`, **98.15 W** (`972.7 W` `1310 nm`) on `Wolf 359`, **357 W** (`GJ 486 b`), **653.6 W** (`LHS 3844 b`), **718 W** (`GJ 1002 b`), and a **1.6237 kW** median across the **103 `< 10 pc` stars** — **0 verified technosignature detections** are claimed; every negative branch, `G4_V38` Fiber-B regression (`577 -> 0` on `D086`), upgraded `COMB_V4_1`, forensic closure (`121 + 3` hits in `D087`), and pure-stdlib numerical audit (`15/15 PASS`) is preserved.*
