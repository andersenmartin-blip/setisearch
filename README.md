# SETIsearch — Reproducerbar arkivbaseret teknosignaturforskning

*English summary: Open, prospectively locked archival technosignature research by Martin Andersen (MSc Astrophysics, University of Copenhagen), spanning panchromatic high-resolution optical, near-infrared YJH, and thermal near-infrared $K$-band echelle spectroscopy across both hemispheres (`0.38–2.48 µm`; ESO HARPS, VLT ESPRESSO, ESO NIRPS, VLT CRIRES+, TNG HARPS-N, Calar Alto CARMENES_VIS & FEROS), multi-epoch planetary-rest-frame `1.4 GHz` narrowband radio searches (`seti-repeater`, Parkes/GBT), and TESS specular light-sail photometry. Across all tracks — culminating in the completed **125-star (`832`-exposure, `180.82 h`, 6-spectrograph) All-Sky Panchromatic `V3.0–V3.5` Survey** (`D079–D084`) and **136-System Multi-Messenger Synthesis** ([`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf) · [`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md) · [`paper/main.tex`](paper/main.tex)), reaching **9.03 W** optically on Proxima Centauri, **73.90 W** (`705 nm`) on DX Cnc, **98.15 W** on Wolf 359, **357 W** orbital `K_PLANET` on GJ 486 b, **653.6 W** orbital `K_PLANET` on LHS 3844 b, and a **1.6237 kW** median across all **53 `< 10 pc` stars** — **0 verified technosignature detections** are claimed; every negative branch, `G6_V35` / dichroic finding, forensic diagnosis, and pure-stdlib numerical audit is preserved.*

## Hovedresultater fra `D084` (`NORTHERN_V3_5`, `125`-Stjerners & `136`-Systems All-Sky Syntese)

1. **Nordlig prospektiv kalibrering (`2.151` forsøg) & automatisk `G6_V35`-afvejning (`10.692` samlet i `D079–D084`):** Over `183` nordlige eksponeringer (`64` `HARPS-N` + `119` `CARMENES_VIS`) opnår `NORTHERN_V3_5` **`91,27 %` (`230/252`)** komplethed i det rene optiske kerneområde (`532–670 nm`, $\ge 8\sigma$), mens den samlede `CARMENES_VIS`-rate (`54,94 %`) afspejler den målte dikroiske afskæring over `~995 nm` (`CARACAL FOX SNR = 0,5–1,2` i orden `60–61`). Keplerisk `K_PLANET` genfinder `20,99 %` ($\ge 6,5\sigma$; `55,56 %` ved `14σ`; `14/36` vs. `0/36` på `Ross 248`) mod `0,00 %` i `T_DIFF`, `G5_V35` afviser `100,0 %` (`72/72`) kosmiske stråler, og den nye automatiske kryds-stjerne **Gate `G6_V35`** opnår **`100,0 %` (`60/60`) sky-OH og `100,0 %` (`60/60`) varm-pixel afvisning** ved `65,0 %` sand laser-komplethed.
2. **Eliminering af `8.063` `Legacy V2` falskalarmer (`1.170` i `D084`) & forensisk afklaring (`12 / 12`):** `G6_V35` eliminerer automatisk **`116 / 128` (`90,6 %`: `115` `TOPO` + `1` `FIXED`)** af præ-`G6` overleverne; de `12` resterende enkelt-epoke `Channel T`-transienter (`0` kontrol, `0` Kanal `P`, `0` orbitale på tværs af `30` kampagner i alt) er entydigt klassificeret i [`FORENSIC_CANDIDATE_AUDIT_D084.json`](progress/2026-11-16-northern-d084-v3-5/FORENSIC_CANDIDATE_AUDIT_D084.json) $\rightarrow$ **`0` verificerede ETI-detektioner**.
