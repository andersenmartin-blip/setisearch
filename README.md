# SETIsearch — Reproducerbar arkivbaseret teknosignaturforskning

*English summary: Open, prospectively locked archival technosignature research by Martin Andersen (MSc Astrophysics, University of Copenhagen), spanning **8.273 unbroken spectral octaves (`0.0905–28.00 µm`)** across **JWST thermal-IR** (`NIRSpec G235H`, `NIRSpec G395H`, `MIRI LRS`, `MIRI MRS Ch1–4`, including Townes `10.60 µm` $\mathrm{CO_2}$ laser & **molecular/greenhouse-gas `CCF` for `NO2`/`N2O`/`CH4+C2H6`/`CO+13CO` & `CF4`/`C2F6`/`SF6`/`NF3`/`CFC-11`**, and **Project Hephaistos `OBJECT_D`/`OBJECT_E`** prop `7199`), space Lyman-limit/FUV/NUV (`FUSE`, `HST/COS G130M`, `HST/STIS E140M/E230M/E230H`), simultaneous 3-arm ESO spectroscopy (`VLT X-shooter` `UVB+VIS+NIR`, `MPG/ESO FEROS`, `VLT FLAMES/GIRAFFE HR`), Gemini-North `GRACES`, ESO 1.2 m Euler `CORALIE`, ground-based near-UV (`UVES_NUV`), optical (`HARPS`, `HARPS-N`, `ESPRESSO HR/UHR`, `OHP SOPHIE`, `OHP ELODIE`, `CFHT ESPaDOnS`), far-red (`UVES_RED`, `CARMENES_VIS`), near-infrared YJHK (`NIRPS_HE`, `SDSS APOGEE-2`, `CRIRES+` $Y/J/H/K$, `CFHT SPIRou`), multi-epoch planetary-rest-frame `1.4 GHz` radio searches (`seti-repeater`, Parkes/GBT), and TESS photometry. Across all sixteen survey phases — culminating in **Phase D094 (`TECHNOSIGNATURE_V5_2_MULTIEPOCH_DRIFT_APOGEE2_CRIRESJH_MIRILRS`)** uniting **373 unique stellar systems** (`2,238` exposures, `702.8369 h`, `23` spectrographs / `29` spectral arms, `41` panchromatic bridges across `664` cells, `156` `< 10 pc` stars with **`1.48898 kW`** median limit, **`34,149` calibration trials**, **`125` orbital campaigns**, `424` uniform all-sky `COMB_V4_1` stacks [`22,694 -> 109 -> 0 ETI`], and **100% [`23/23`] Parkes/GBT 1.4 GHz radio-echelle closure** [`24` direct overlaps incl. tri-regime `16CYGB` and `GJ849`]) ([`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf) · [`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md) · [`paper/main.tex`](paper/main.tex)) — `V5.2` multi-epoch linear drift chaining (`DRIFT_V52_MULTIEPOCH`; `59/60` recovery at `0/90` FA across `44` $N\ge 3$ campaigns [`13 D094 + 31 D091 Archive Q` -> `0 ETI`]), Gate `G8_V52` (`40/40` isolated pass at `0/80` FA; `947` V2 FA $\rightarrow 81 \rightarrow 16 \rightarrow 1 \rightarrow 0$ ETI), union-excluded `JWST MIRI LRS` (`77/80` at `0/80` FA; `0/10` detections) & `NIRSpec G395H` (`60/60` at `0/60` FA; `0/2` detections), `54/54` `COMB_V4_1` (`0/108` forest/topo FA), `120/120` agnostic multi-scale recovery (`100` natural -> `0 ETI`), and `373`-star 3-event SETI Ellipsoid / `ETZ` synchronization (`10` `ETZ`, `34` `<= 180 d`) establish **0 verified technosignature detections** across all **`373` stars (`303,145` V2 false alarms eliminated; `16/16 PASS` pure-stdlib audit; `33/33` clean-room bit-identity)**.*
