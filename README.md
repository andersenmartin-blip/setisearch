# SETIsearch — Reproducerbar arkivbaseret teknosignaturforskning

*English summary: Open, prospectively locked archival technosignature research by Martin Andersen (MSc Astrophysics, University of Copenhagen), spanning more than four spectral octaves (`0.1145–2.515 µm`, `4.457` octaves) across space far-UV (`HST_STIS E140M`), ground-based near-UV (`UVES_NUV`), optical (`HARPS`, `HARPS-N`, `ESPRESSO HR/UHR`, `OHP SOPHIE`, `OHP ELODIE`, `CFHT ESPaDOnS`), far-red (`UVES_RED`, `CARMENES_VIS`), near-infrared YJH (`NIRPS_HE`), thermal $K$-band (`CRIRES+`), and simultaneous YJHK (`CFHT SPIRou`) echelle laser & frequency-comb spectroscopy across both hemispheres and space (alongside `FEROS`), multi-epoch planetary-rest-frame `1.4 GHz` narrowband radio searches (`seti-repeater`, Parkes/GBT), and TESS specular light-sail photometry. Across all tracks — culminating in the completed **220-star (`1,567`-exposure, `365.80 h`, 12-spectrograph / 13-arm) All-Sky & Space Panchromatic `D079–D088` Survey** with **100% (`23/23`) Parkes/GBT 1.4 GHz radio-echelle closure** (`24` direct overlaps) and **uniform all-sky `COMB_V4_1` execution across 100% of `D079–D088` (`247` stacks / `1,567` exposures)** ([`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf) · [`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md) · [`paper/main.tex`](paper/main.tex)), reaching **0.0973 W (`97.3 mW`)** in space far-UV (`1275 Å` `HST_STIS`), **0.80 W** in the near-UV (`3180 Å` `UVES_NUV`), **0.5181 kW** (`SOPHIE GJ 625`), **1.0796 kW** (`ESPaDOnS GJ 687`), and **9.03 W** optically on Proxima Centauri, and a **1.7757 kW** median across **117 `< 10 pc` stars** — **0 verified technosignature detections** are claimed; every negative branch, `G4_V38` Fiber-B regression (`577 -> 0` on `D086`), uniform all-sky `COMB_V4_1` (`5,434 -> 15 -> 0 ETI`), forensic closure (`20` post-`G6` in `D088` + `15` all-sky `COMB_V4_1`), and pure-stdlib numerical audit (`16/16 PASS`) is preserved.*
