# SETIsearch — Reproducerbar arkivbaseret teknosignaturforskning

*English summary: Open, prospectively locked archival technosignature research by Martin Andersen (MSc Astrophysics, University of Copenhagen), spanning **8.273 unbroken spectral octaves (`0.0905–28.00 µm`)** across **JWST thermal-IR** (`NIRSpec G235H`, `NIRSpec G395H`, `MIRI MRS Ch1–4`, including Townes `10.60 µm` $\mathrm{CO_2}$ laser & **greenhouse-gas `CCF` for `CF4`/`C2F6`/`SF6`/`NF3`**, and **Project Hephaistos `OBJECT_D`/`OBJECT_E`** prop `7199`), space Lyman-limit/FUV/NUV (`FUSE`, `HST/COS G130M`, `HST/STIS E140M/E230M/E230H`), simultaneous 3-arm ESO spectroscopy (`VLT X-shooter` `UVB+VIS+NIR`, `MPG/ESO FEROS`, `VLT FLAMES/GIRAFFE HR`), ground-based near-UV (`UVES_NUV`), optical (`HARPS`, `HARPS-N`, `ESPRESSO HR/UHR`, `OHP SOPHIE`, `OHP ELODIE`, `CFHT ESPaDOnS`), far-red (`UVES_RED`, `CARMENES_VIS`), near-infrared YJHK (`NIRPS_HE`, `CRIRES+` $Y$ & $K$, `CFHT SPIRou`), multi-epoch planetary-rest-frame `1.4 GHz` radio searches (`seti-repeater`, Parkes/GBT), and TESS photometry. Across all fourteen survey phases — culminating in **Phase D092 (`TECHNOSIGNATURE_V5_0_RPCA_RARITY_ELLIPSOID_PPO_MIRI_CCF`)** uniting **335 unique stellar systems** (`2,133` exposures, `647.05 h`, `19` spectrographs / `22` spectral arms, `37` panchromatic bridges across `631` cells, `146` `< 10 pc` stars with **`1.5730 kW`** median limit, **`30,817` calibration trials**, **`109` orbital campaigns**, `386` uniform all-sky `COMB_V4_1` stacks [`22,691 -> 109 -> 0 ETI`], and **100% [`23/23`] Parkes/GBT 1.4 GHz radio-echelle closure** [`24` direct overlaps incl. tri-regime `16CYGB` and `GJ849`]) ([`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf) · [`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md) · [`paper/main.tex`](paper/main.tex)) — V5.0 unsupervised masked RPCA + cross-star Rarity (`G7_V50`) retroactively suppresses **`98.815%`** of D091 V4.2 survivors (`2,362 -> 28`), trims `JWST MIRI MRS` dichroic filter roll-offs (`0` disk-quiet/control greenhouse flags across `60` cells), explores `5` `ETZ`, `6` `< 10 pc` `SN 1987A Ellipsoid`, and `9` `PPO` synchronization targets (`0` ETI), and runs `14,274` dual-polarity agnostic multi-scale evaluations (`107 -> 0 ETI`), establishing **0 verified technosignature detections** across all **`335` stars (`301,190` V2 false alarms eliminated; `16/16 PASS` pure-stdlib audit; `29/29` clean-room bit-identity)**.*
