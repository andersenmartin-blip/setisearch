# SETIsearch — Reproducerbar arkivbaseret teknosignaturforskning

*English summary: Open, prospectively locked archival technosignature research by Martin Andersen (MSc Astrophysics, University of Copenhagen), spanning **8.709 unbroken spectral octaves (`0.0905–37.86356 µm`)** across **JWST** (`NIRISS/SOSS` `0.68–2.83 µm`, `NIRSpec/G140H` `0.95–1.88 µm`, `NIRSpec G235H/G395H`, `MIRI LRS`, `MIRI MRS Ch1–4`, including Townes `10.60 µm` $\mathrm{CO_2}$ laser, molecular/greenhouse-gas `CCF`, and Project Hephaistos `OBJECT_D`/`OBJECT_E`), airborne high-resolution mid-IR echelon-echelle (**`SOFIA/EXES`** `5.62–26.13 µm`, $R \approx 86{.}000$), `Spitzer IRS SL+LL` (`5.20–37.86356 µm`), space Lyman-limit/FUV/NUV (`FUSE`, `HST/COS G130M`, `HST/STIS E140M/E230M/E230H`), simultaneous 3-arm ESO spectroscopy (`VLT X-shooter` `UVB+VIS+NIR`, `MPG/ESO FEROS`, `VLT FLAMES/GIRAFFE HR`), Gemini-South `GHOST` (simultaneous sky-IFU veto), Gemini-North `GRACES`, ESO `CORALIE`, ground near-UV (`UVES_NUV`), optical (`HARPS`, `HARPS-N`, `ESPRESSO HR/UHR`, `OHP SOPHIE`, `OHP ELODIE`, `CFHT ESPaDOnS`), far-red (`UVES_RED`, `CARMENES_VIS` with per-exposure CARACAL `SERVAL` RV alignment), near-infrared YJHK (`NIRPS_HE`, `SDSS APOGEE-2` up to `N=28` visits, `CRIRES+`, `CFHT SPIRou`), multi-epoch planetary-rest-frame `1.4 GHz` radio searches (`seti-repeater`, Parkes/GBT), and `TESS` photometry. Across all eighteen survey phases — culminating in **Phase D096 (`V5.4`: `TECHNOSIGNATURE_V5_4_SOFIA_EXES_MIDIR_ECHELLE_COHERENT_DRIFT_AND_JWST_NIRISS`)** uniting **425 unique stellar systems** (`2,523` exposures, `751.6534 h`, `27` spectrographs / `36` spectral arms, `52` calibrated panchromatic bridges across `719` cells [`+ BD+60 1753 = 53` multi-phase targets], `165` `< 10 pc` stars with **`1.5222 kW`** median limit, **`37,911` calibration trials**, **`138` orbital campaigns**, `448` uniform all-sky `COMB_V4_1` stacks [`HNLIB 85 -> 0`; `0 ETI`], and **100% [`23/23`] Parkes/GBT 1.4 GHz radio-echelle closure** [`24` direct overlaps]) ([`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf) · [`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md) · [`paper/main.tex`](paper/main.tex)) — `SOFIA/EXES` (`17` stars / `348` orders -> `149 G1 -> 148 G45 -> 1 G6 -> 0 ETI`; `59/60` at `0/120` FA), NaN-preserving coherent shift-stacking (`DRIFT_V54`; `57/60` curved, `54/60` periodic at `0/120` FA across `20` $N\ge 4$ campaigns / `13,538` trajectories / `203,752,384` pixel integrals -> `765 -> 0 ETI` via exact-RV topocentric $\mathrm{CO_2}$/OH recurrence & epoch-subset support), `JWST NIRISS/SOSS + NIRSpec/G140H` (`57/60` at `0/60` FA; `19 -> 0 ETI` via deterministic Stage-2 `J1–J5` triage), `BLIND_DRIFT_V51` (`17 -> 0`), `DRIFT_V52` (`17 -> 0`), and `425`-star SETI Ellipsoid/`ETZ` (`15` `ETZ`, `36 <= 180 d`) establish **0 verified technosignature detections** down to **`0.039712 kW` (`39.71 W`, `JWST_NIRISS_SOSS` `SIMP 0136`)**, **`0.114498 kW` (`CARMENES LP 731-58`)**, **`34.509826 kW` (`JWST_NIRSPEC_G140H VHS 1256 b`)**, and **`202.381291 kW` (`SOFIA_EXES` `Sirius` `7.21 µm`)** across all **`425` stars (`313,257` V2 false alarms eliminated; `16/16 PASS` pure-stdlib audit; `36/36` clean-room bit-identity; Companion Archive `V` `15.20 MiB`)**.*
