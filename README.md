# SETIsearch — Reproducerbar arkivbaseret teknosignaturforskning

*English summary: Open, prospectively locked archival technosignature research by Martin Andersen (MSc Astrophysics, University of Copenhagen), spanning panchromatic high-resolution optical, near-infrared YJH, and thermal near-infrared $K$-band echelle spectroscopy (`0.38–2.48 µm`; ESO HARPS, VLT ESPRESSO, ESO NIRPS, VLT CRIRES+ & FEROS), multi-epoch planetary-rest-frame `1.4 GHz` narrowband radio searches (`seti-repeater`, Parkes/GBT), and TESS specular light-sail photometry. Across all tracks — culminating in the completed **100-star (`649`-exposure, `154.11 h`) Panchromatic `V3.0–V3.4` Survey** (`D079–D083`) and **120-System Multi-Messenger Synthesis** ([`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf) · [`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md) · [`paper/main.tex`](paper/main.tex)), reaching **9.03 W** optically and **607.7 W** at `1064.1 nm` (`1,026.1 W` at `1550.0 nm`) on Proxima Centauri and **653.6 W** orbital `K_PLANET` (`1,513.2 W` single-epoch; `1,557.7 W` at `2097.0 nm` Ho:YAG) on LHS 3844 — **0 verified technosignature detections** are claimed; every negative branch, detector/airglow diagnosis, and pure-stdlib numerical audit is preserved.*

## Hovedresultater fra `D083` (`CRIRES_V3_4` $K$-Båndssurvey, `100`-Stjerners & `120`-Systems Syntese)

1. **Termisk nær-infrarød 3-Hawaii-2RG & Keplerisk de-Doppler kalibrering (`1.971` forsøg; `8.541` samlet i `D079–D083`):** På `148` $R=86\,000$ VLT `CRIRES+`-eksponeringer (`1,95–2,48 µm`) opnår `CRIRES_V3_4` **`83,33 %`** komplethed ved **`2040,0 nm` (`Tm`-fiber)** og **`94,44 %`** ved **`2097,0 nm` (`Ho:YAG`)** ($\ge 8\sigma$; `88,89 %` samlet ved $\ge 12\sigma$ i `Channel P`), løfter Keplerisk planet-hvileramme genfinding over $\Delta v_{\mathrm{orb}} = 224,4\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ fra `0,00 %` til **`53,09 %`** ($\ge 6,5\sigma_{\mathrm{exp}}$), og opnår **`98,61 %` (`71/72`) ægte slids-laser transmission mod `100,00 %` (`144/144`) afvisning** af 3-H2RG `IPC` hot-pixels og persistens-plateauer (`Gate G5_V34`).
2. **Eliminering af `6.893` `Legacy V2` falskalarmer (`1.043` i `D083`) & forensisk høj-$J$ OH-diagnose:** På tværs af alle `20` orbitale transit/konjunktions- og kontrolkampagner i `D081–D083` (`222` eksponeringer) returnerer både `Channel T_DIFF` og `Channel K_PLANET` **`0` kandidater**. I `D083` diagnosticeres samtlige `17` `Channel T/P`-overlevere i [`FORENSIC_KBAND_CASCADE_DIAGNOSIS_21.json`](progress/2026-11-15-crires-d083-v3-4/FORENSIC_KBAND_CASCADE_DIAGNOSIS_21.json) som `15` topocentriske høj-$J$ OH Meinel $\Lambda$-dubletter / faste H2RG-pixels delt af `2–13` urelaterede stjerner, `1` cirkumstellar Br-$\gamma$ eksokomet-vinge på $\beta$ Pictoris (`-86,8 km/s`) og `1` kontinuum-skulder på `PDS 70` → **`0` verificerede ETI-lasere**.
3. **Sub-kilowatt til kilowatt grænser over `0,38–2,48 µm` & `120`-systems multi-messenger syntese:** Komplette kataloger foreligger i [`UNIFIED_PANCHROMATIC_V3_SYNTHESIS_100STARS.csv`](progress/2026-11-15-crires-d083-v3-4/UNIFIED_PANCHROMATIC_V3_SYNTHESIS_100STARS.csv), [`SOLAR_NEIGHBORHOOD_10PC_PANCHROMATIC_35STARS.csv`](progress/2026-11-15-crires-d083-v3-4/SOLAR_NEIGHBORHOOD_10PC_PANCHROMATIC_35STARS.csv), [`PANCHROMATIC_OPTICAL_NIR_KBAND_BRIDGE.csv`](progress/2026-11-15-crires-d083-v3-4/PANCHROMATIC_OPTICAL_NIR_KBAND_BRIDGE.csv) og [`MULTIMESSENGER_ECHELLE_RADIO_TESS_SYNTHESIS.csv`](progress/2026-11-15-crires-d083-v3-4/MULTIMESSENGER_ECHELLE_RADIO_TESS_SYNTHESIS.csv).
