# SETIsearch — Reproducerbar arkivbaseret teknosignaturforskning

*English summary: Open, prospectively locked archival technosignature research by Martin Andersen (MSc Astrophysics, University of Copenhagen), spanning **8.273 unbroken spectral octaves (`0.0905–28.00 µm`)** across **JWST thermal-IR** (`NIRSpec G235H`, `NIRSpec G395H`, `MIRI MRS Ch1–4`, including Townes `10.60 µm` $\mathrm{CO_2}$ laser & **molecular/greenhouse-gas `CCF` for `NO2`/`N2O`/`CH4+C2H6`/`CO+13CO` & `CF4`/`C2F6`/`SF6`/`NF3`**, and **Project Hephaistos `OBJECT_D`/`OBJECT_E`** prop `7199`), space Lyman-limit/FUV/NUV (`FUSE`, `HST/COS G130M`, `HST/STIS E140M/E230M/E230H`), simultaneous 3-arm ESO spectroscopy (`VLT X-shooter` `UVB+VIS+NIR`, `MPG/ESO FEROS`, `VLT FLAMES/GIRAFFE HR`), Gemini-North `GRACES`, ESO 1.2 m Euler `CORALIE`, ground-based near-UV (`UVES_NUV`), optical (`HARPS`, `HARPS-N`, `ESPRESSO HR/UHR`, `OHP SOPHIE`, `OHP ELODIE`, `CFHT ESPaDOnS`), far-red (`UVES_RED`, `CARMENES_VIS`), near-infrared YJHK (`NIRPS_HE`, `CRIRES+` $Y$ & $K$, `CFHT SPIRou`), multi-epoch planetary-rest-frame `1.4 GHz` radio searches (`seti-repeater`, Parkes/GBT), and TESS photometry. Across all fifteen survey phases — culminating in **Phase D093 (`TECHNOSIGNATURE_V5_1_DEDOPPLER_DRIFT_MULTIEVENT_ELLIPSOID_GRACES_CORALIE_CARMENES`)** uniting **350 unique stellar systems** (`2,163` exposures, `652.71 h`, `21` spectrographs / `24` spectral arms, `37` panchromatic bridges across `631` cells, `153` `< 10 pc` stars with **`1.45576 kW`** median limit, **`32,408` calibration trials**, **`117` orbital campaigns**, `401` uniform all-sky `COMB_V4_1` stacks [`22,694 -> 109 -> 0 ETI`], and **100% [`23/23`] Parkes/GBT 1.4 GHz radio-echelle closure** [`24` direct overlaps incl. tri-regime `16CYGB` and `GJ849`]) ([`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf) · [`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md) · [`paper/main.tex`](paper/main.tex)) — `V5.1` blind linear drift-rate de-Doppler (`DRIFT_V51`; `60/60` recovery, `111,520,615` empirical evals -> `135` pairs -> `0 ETI`), Gate `G8_V51b` standardized patch novelty (`39/40` isolated pass at `0/80` confounders; `95.0%` retroactive `D088` suppression `20 -> 13 -> 1 -> 0 ETI`), union-excluded `JWST NIRSpec G395H` CCF (`80/80` recovery at `0/80` confounders; `0/12` control flags across `44` cells), `120/120` agnostic multi-scale recovery (`3 -> 0 ETI`), and `350`-star 3-event SETI Ellipsoid / `ETZ` synchronization (`10` `ETZ`, `33` `<= 180 d`) establish **0 verified technosignature detections** across all **`350` stars (`302,198` V2 false alarms eliminated; `16/16 PASS` pure-stdlib audit; `33/33` clean-room bit-identity)**.*
