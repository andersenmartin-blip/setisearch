# SETIsearch — Reproducerbar arkivbaseret teknosignaturforskning

*English summary: Open, prospectively locked archival technosignature research by Martin Andersen (MSc Astrophysics, University of Copenhagen), spanning panchromatic high-resolution optical, near-infrared YJH, thermal $K$-band, and simultaneous YJHK echelle laser & frequency-comb spectroscopy across both hemispheres (`0.38–2.515 µm`; ESO HARPS, VLT ESPRESSO, ESO NIRPS, VLT CRIRES+, TNG HARPS-N, Calar Alto CARMENES_VIS, CFHT 3.6 m SPIRou, and FEROS), multi-epoch planetary-rest-frame `1.4 GHz` narrowband radio searches (`seti-repeater`, Parkes/GBT), and TESS specular light-sail photometry. Across all tracks — culminating in the completed **150-star (`1,009`-exposure, `195.23 h`, 7-spectrograph) All-Sky Panchromatic `V3.0–V4.0` Survey** (`D079–D085`) and **160-System Multi-Messenger Synthesis** ([`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf) · [`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md) · [`paper/main.tex`](paper/main.tex)), reaching **9.03 W** optically on Proxima Centauri, **73.90 W** (`705 nm`) and **730.5 W** (`1310 nm` YJHK) on DX Cnc, **98.15 W** (`972.7 W` `1310 nm`) on Wolf 359, **357 W** orbital `K_PLANET` on GJ 486 b, **653.6 W** orbital `K_PLANET` on LHS 3844 b, **718 W** orbital on GJ 1002 b, and a **2.3716 kW** median across all **73 `< 10 pc` stars** — **0 verified technosignature detections** are claimed; every negative branch, sparse `G6_V36` (`95.0 %`), `COMB_V4` (`97.2 %`), forensic closure (`113 + 19` hits), and pure-stdlib numerical audit is preserved.*

## Hovedresultater fra `D085` (`SPIROU_V3_6_AND_COMB_V4`, `150`-Stjerners & `160`-Systems All-Sky Syntese)

1. **Sparsomt Gate `G6_V36` (`95,0 %`), Kanal 5 `COMB_V4` (`97,22 %`) & H4RG Dual-Fiber (`2.592` Forsøg):** Over `177` CFHT SPIRou eksponeringer (`49 × 4088`, `0,955–2,515 µm`, APERO `0.7.295`) opnår `SPIROU_V3_6` **`85,49 %`** Kanal `T` ($\ge 8\sigma$; **`95,8 %`** ved `1064,1 nm` og `1662,0 nm`), **`85,80 %` / `90,28 %`** Kanal `P` ($9\sigma/12\sigma$), **`25,93 %`** vs. `0,00 %` Keplerisk `K_PLANET`, og **`97,22 %`** H4RG + `FluxA/FluxB` laser-pass ved **`100,0 %`** IPC/plateau-afvisning. Komprimering af `G6` til `4.472` supraphotosfæriske toppe (`11,154×` mod `49.880`) hæver sand laser-gennemgang fra `50,0 %` (`V3.5`) til **`95,0 %`** (`100 %` OH-, varmpixel- & BERV-stationær afvisning), mens **`COMB_V4`** (`20,92M` konfigurationer) genfinder **`97,22 %`** af 6-tands kamme ($\ge 3,8\sigma$/tand) med `0,00 %` (`0/48`) enkeltlinje-FPR og `2,22 %` (`1/45`) pseudo-kam-FPR.
2. **Eliminering af `12.107` `Legacy V2` falskalarmer (`4.044` i `D085`) & fuld forensisk afklaring (`113 + 19`):** Sparsomt `G6_V36` eliminerer `166 / 279` præ-`G6` kandidater; samtlige `113` enkeltlinje-overlevere (`76` i `5` tidlige 2018 5-HDU filer uden `FluxA/B`, `22` sub-tærskel 1-stjerne telluriske/OH, `14` sæson-stationære `|ΔBERV| < 3,5 km/s`, `1` isoleret transient) samt alle `19` `COMB_V4`-kandidater (`17` sub-kontinuum M2V FeH/TiO-krusninger på `Lalande 21185` + `2` $\mathrm{O_2}/\mathrm{H_2O}$ tellurisk kant / `Al I` på $\rho$\,CrB) er entydigt klassificeret i [`FORENSIC_CANDIDATE_AUDIT_D085.json`](progress/2026-11-17-spirou-d085-v3-6-comb-v4/FORENSIC_CANDIDATE_AUDIT_D085.json) $\rightarrow$ **`0` verificerede ETI-detektioner**.
3. **`73`-stjerners `< 10 pc` census (`2,3716 kW` median), `8`-stjerners bro (`155` celler) & `160`-systems (`14`-overlap) syntese:** Se [`UNIFIED_PANCHROMATIC_V3_SYNTHESIS_150STARS.csv`](progress/2026-11-17-spirou-d085-v3-6-comb-v4/results/UNIFIED_PANCHROMATIC_V3_SYNTHESIS_150STARS.csv), [`SOLAR_NEIGHBORHOOD_10PC_PANCHROMATIC_73STARS.csv`](progress/2026-11-17-spirou-d085-v3-6-comb-v4/results/SOLAR_NEIGHBORHOOD_10PC_PANCHROMATIC_73STARS.csv), [`PANCHROMATIC_OPTICAL_NIR_K_BRIDGE_8STARS.csv`](progress/2026-11-17-spirou-d085-v3-6-comb-v4/results/PANCHROMATIC_OPTICAL_NIR_K_BRIDGE_8STARS.csv) og [`MULTIMESSENGER_ECHELLE_RADIO_TESS_160SYSTEMS.csv`](progress/2026-11-17-spirou-d085-v3-6-comb-v4/results/MULTIMESSENGER_ECHELLE_RADIO_TESS_160SYSTEMS.csv).
