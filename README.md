# SETIsearch — Reproducerbar arkivbaseret teknosignaturforskning

*English summary: Open, prospectively locked archival technosignature research by Martin Andersen (MSc Astrophysics, University of Copenhagen), spanning **26.086 total spectral octaves (`0.00012–8,556.41831 µm`)** — uniting **9.186 strictly unbroken high-energy octaves (`0.00012–0.06998 µm`)** across *Chandra* `HETG`/`LETG` and *EUVE* `DS/S` up to the neutral H ISM photoionization wall (`699.8–905 Å`) with **13.935 strict zero-gap Lyman-to-mm octaves (`0.0905–1,417.76119 µm`; `14.693` octaves to ALMA Band 4 low `2,397.29 µm`)** and Band 1 (`8,556.41831 µm`) across **Chandra**, **EUVE**, **ALMA** (`Bands 1, 3–8`), **JCMT**, **Herschel**, **SOFIA**, **ISO/SWS**, **JWST**, **Spitzer**, **IRTF**, space UV (**FUSE**, **IUE**, **HST**), VLT/ESO (`X-shooter`, `ESPRESSO`, `HARPS`, `UVES`, `FEROS`, `GIRAFFE`, `NIRPS_HE`, `CRIRES+`), Gemini (`GHOST`/`GRACES`), `CORALIE`, `HARPS-N`, `OHP SOPHIE/ELODIE`, `CFHT ESPaDOnS/SPIRou`, `CARMENES_VIS`, `SDSS APOGEE-2`, `1.4 GHz` Parkes/GBT radio (`seti-repeater`), and `TESS` photometry.
