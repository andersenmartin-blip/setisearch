# SETIsearch — Reproducerbar arkivbaseret teknosignaturforskning

*English summary: Open, prospectively locked archival technosignature research by Martin Andersen (MSc Astrophysics, University of Copenhagen), spanning more than three spectral octaves across panchromatic near-UV, optical, near-infrared YJH, thermal $K$-band, and simultaneous YJHK echelle laser & frequency-comb spectroscopy across both hemispheres (`0.305–2.515 µm`; VLT `UVES_NUV`, ESO `HARPS`, VLT `ESPRESSO`, ESO `NIRPS`, VLT `CRIRES+`, TNG `HARPS-N`, Calar Alto `CARMENES_VIS`, CFHT 3.6 m `SPIRou`, and `FEROS`), multi-epoch planetary-rest-frame `1.4 GHz` narrowband radio searches (`seti-repeater`, Parkes/GBT), and TESS specular light-sail photometry. Across all tracks — culminating in the completed **175-star (`1,190`-exposure, `252.25 h`, 8-spectrograph) All-Sky Panchromatic `D079–D086` Survey** and **181-System Multi-Messenger Synthesis** ([`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.pdf) · [`paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md`](paper/ANDERSEN_2026_PANCHROMATIC_ECHELLE_SETI_SURVEY.md) · [`paper/main.tex`](paper/main.tex)), reaching **0.80 W** in the near-UV (`3180 Å` `UVES_NUV`) and **9.03 W** optically on Proxima Centauri (now tri-regime NUV+Optical+NIR), **13.69 W** on GJ 1061, **73.90 W** (`705 nm`) and **730.5 W** (`1310 nm` YJHK) on DX Cnc, **98.15 W** (`972.7 W` `1310 nm`) on Wolf 359, **357 W** orbital `K_PLANET` on GJ 486 b, **653.6 W** orbital `K_PLANET` on LHS 3844 b, **718 W** orbital on GJ 1002 b, and a **2.0983 kW** median across the **90 `< 10 pc` stars** — **0 verified technosignature detections** are claimed; every negative branch, upgraded `COMB_V4_1` (`19 -> 0` on `D085` SPIRou false alarms), forensic closure (`865 + 6` hits in `D086`), and pure-stdlib numerical audit (`15/15 PASS`) is preserved.*
