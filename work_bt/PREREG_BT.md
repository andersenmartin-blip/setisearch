# BT — isolated evidence closure

BS recorded recreated pending names, random-suffix temporary files and
`.rsync-tmp` events in the shared workspace. The responsible process and the
historical BQ/BR mechanism remain unestablished. The mitigation under test is
to keep active output in an exclusive, verified tmpfs task directory, finish
all computations and source checks there, then export one closed ZIP whose
complete membership, CRCs, byte counts and SHA256 hashes are verified before
and after export. No earlier execution or failed status is altered.

Two fixed once-only identities are preregistered together:

1. `fixture`: unchanged BR publication on two artificial BS JSONL streams of
   45,000 records each, using their already fixed independent expected hashes.
2. `a04`: conditional on independent acceptance of the fixture's exported
   archive and process receipt, one new completion of the frozen BP A04 saved
   associations with the unchanged BQ association functions and BR closure.
   This opens saved synthetic scores only. No RNG, powers generation, new
   scan scoring, normalization, threshold changes or astronomical powers.

The second identity is not an automatic retry of the old failed identity.
It has a prospectively public source freeze, explicit fixture gate, separate
exclusive output directory and independent final qualification. BP remains
FAIL, BQ remains EVIDENCE_FAIL, BR remains FAIL, BS BR-serial remains FAIL.
Existing complete current and legacy byte hashes are deterministic
predictions for checking the new complete pair, never material from which
the new streams may be assembled. Both streams must be produced by the same
new A04 execution and its frozen computation must pass.

Each trial is launched in one executor command with an initial wait of
30 seconds. No other executor command or agent filesystem work may overlap
it. If it outlasts that wait, only polling the same session is permitted and
must be recorded as a deviation from the intended uninterrupted launch.
Active output remains in tmpfs even in that event. No loop until success.

The parent verifies the exported ZIP, binds its inner result to every full
stream, verifies all source/input pins and reconciles its final inventory
with the child result. Independent review must then verify every archive
member, exact fixture contents or A04 endpoints, resource and process
receipts, and the final public delivery. Export preservation cannot make a
failed computation pass. Any failed or incomplete evidence stays failed.

Fixed per-trial resource limits and source/input hashes are recorded in
BT_ISOLATED_PLAN.json. Maximum two executions, one for each identity. The
full 839-rate control, DATA admission, new archive requests, observations and
astronomical search remain outside this stage.
