# Fresh synthetic controls — definitions prepared 8 October 2026

These files define a new independent development and validation experiment.
No random arrays, validation outcomes or pilot spectra have been generated by
preparing these files. Root publishes and checks the source/config/seed freeze
before admitting the DEV-only invocation. Validation A is not a development
sample; after an allowed development correction, validation B supplies wholly
fresh random identities. Old 112+128/127+24 identities and outcomes are unused.

`generator.py` is imported without drawing random values. Its command-line
interface writes definitions and SHA256 seed identities only. `generate_case`
returns six native-order synthetic power arrays and separately labelled truth.
Truth must never be passed to the detector. `summarize.py` reads already-observed
outcomes; it does not generate arrays or run the detector.

Main controls use independent positive Gamma power with shape 16, mean 1 and
standard deviation .25. Noise-only controls additionally declare Gaussian
residual power, row-gain Gamma power, and frequency AR(1) Gaussian residuals.
Gaussian draws are checked for finite positive power; invalid draws fail the
case without clipping or resampling. All laws are synthetic and do not establish
an empirical sky/RFI false-alarm rate.

The signal is an intrinsic uniform line, one or three native channels wide,
convolved with its uniform frequency sweep over each complete integration.
Exact CDF differences assign frequency-bin power. At 4 Hz/s and 17.986224128 s
integration, the sweep alone spans about 25.37 native channels. Actual scan
start times and their gaps are used, with one continuous absolute trajectory.
The reference frequency is placed at the whole-cadence reference time.

For each active scan, the deterministic injection flux is chosen before noise
values are generated. Its frequency-summed mean-integration power is
`A = target * .25 / B`, where `B` is the largest noiseless, unfiltered,
true-centred box projection among widths 1/3/9/33. The denominator is
`sqrt(16 * width)`. Target 24 is used for strong controls; target 12 for
operating controls. These are declared-law ideal projections, not the eventual
robust detector score, measured physical flux or telescope S/N. Profiles and
noise go through the complete production preprocessing and ON/OFF comparison.
Box centres use `numpy.rint` on absolute source-channel indices, including
ties-to-even, before subtracting the first loaded source channel.

The full validation panel has 14 strong signals (all seven nonempty ON activity
subsets at both extreme drift rates), 48 operating signals, 24 matched ON/OFF
interference cadences, and 32 independent noise cadences. Four interference
cadences include a stronger incompatible OFF-only nuisance, to expose a detector
that loses the compatible OFF evidence by retaining only its largest peak.
An additional 12 single-row ON transients and 12 nearby-OFF contamination cases
are descriptive diagnostics, outside eligibility counts. Each validation panel
therefore has 142 case identities; the DEV panel has 24 representative cases.

Recovery requires a surviving ON track localized to the injected reference
frequency and drift under the separately frozen tolerance rule. Both any-ON
and all-active-ON recovery are recorded. Eligibility requires a correctly
localized surviving hit in every active ON scan, with subgroup limits. Strong
recovery requires 14/14; operating requires at least 44/48, at least 7/8 in each
activity pattern, 10/12 at each drift rate, and 22/24 at each intrinsic width.
Every matched-interference cadence must first detect the injected primary track
in all three ON scans before OFF vetoing. A missed injected signal cannot be
counted as successful RFI rejection. No matched-interference cadence may retain
any ON candidate; no more than one
of the 32 noise cadences may retain a candidate. Missing, failed or duplicate
case outcomes cannot become an empty scientific result or a passing gate.

All scripts, metadata templates, case definitions, detector settings and
normalization rules must be frozen together before their admitted invocation.
The historical metadata template does not grant present-day source admission.
