## One bounded diagnosis on retained evidence

The [diagnosis scope](RADIO_HD189733_NULL_SUPPORT_2026-09-28_SCOPE.md) was written
before that investigation. It loaded the three retained lossless score archives
and verified **3,888 complete vector payloads and ancestry identities**, then
reproduced every frozen mask hash. An independent boolean enumeration, using
neither `stack_hypothesis` nor the calibration accumulator, compared eligibility
for all 381 published shifted samples and all three observed alignments.
**All empty/nonempty results agree exactly.**

No calibration mask cell was excluded. The absence therefore comes from the
fixed requirement that at least two active epochs simultaneously exceed S/N 3
at a compatible scored carrier. The three realizations contain only 240, 314
and 116 above-floor epoch score cells across the 81-template/eight-width scored
inventory; these cells are correlated and are not independent noise samples.
Their shifted coincidence counts range from 0–5, 0–3 and 0–1 respectively.
Complete counts by template, width and shift, all empty-shift identities, and
maximum epoch scores are retained in
[`diagnosis01`](results_radio_hd189733_panel_2026-09-28/diagnosis01).

This distinguishes a legitimate **no-eligible-hypothesis probability mass** from
missing computation, lost records or a metadata-schema crash. The currently
frozen certificate requires finite maxima for every conditional row, so these
realizations cannot produce that certificate. It is not legitimate to discard
empty rows and call the remaining rows a qualified false-alarm tail, or to
reinterpret the empty observations as a measured zero false-alarm rate.

The missing method capability is a prospectively justified calibration contract
that explicitly handles the no-eligible-hypothesis outcome. Any such replacement
must have a newly authorized, disjoint calibration/evaluation allocation and
its own prospective scope; this failed attempt supplies development/diagnosis
evidence only. No repair, new threshold, wider bank, extra noise realization or
retest is authorized by the current zero-remedy budget. The plan's one bounded
diagnosis for this failure is now consumed and closed.
