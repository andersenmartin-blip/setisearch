Prepared 9 September 2026 after reading the completed M43AB outcomes.
This is a retrospective diagnostic, not a new endpoint or independent test.
The source is public M43AB result commit
`2612645426a348bcbf02917c110751e24671be57`; all original sealed input records
and their checksums must match its result inventory before analysis.

## Questions and fixed scope

1. Summarize original alias rejections and centered releases on all 149 stored
   inputs, keeping signal/control and historical/fresh denominators separate.
   Trace the old best witness on released members: did centered sampling lose
   two above-floor shared epochs, or lose the existing 20 Hz peak agreement?
   This describes that witness, not every possible pair in the alias search.
2. Preserve both signatures, width and predicted ON track span on the recovered
   170/171 and 280/281 families, control 96, broad ON/OFF control 138 and fresh032.
   Include the other three recovered histories and weak family 260/261/265.
   Compare peak displacement with the filter half span and the track envelope.
   These geometric descriptors are not a proposed or applied veto.
3. Reconstruct ON/OFF and baseline response neighborhoods for the single
   new-combination survivor in z138 and fresh032, and fresh032's original best
   alias witness. Use radius 160 proxy bins and all eight existing widths,
   reusing the M43AA response reader and its explicit native arithmetic checks.
   Verify the sealed M43AB center scores, overlay identity, fixed correlation
   evidence and both receiver signatures at the focal members.
4. Report raw and injected-minus-baseline shapes separately. Compare their
   centered correlations at the original width, without fitting shifts,
   choosing a new threshold or modifying an earlier classification.
