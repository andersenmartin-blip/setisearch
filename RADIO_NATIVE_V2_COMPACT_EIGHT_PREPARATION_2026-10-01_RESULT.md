## Open start blockers

The exact prospective plan is authoritative for the blocker list. Independent
review identified these necessary joins before any large input is permitted:

- Complete public code, derived-helper, input and runtime closure, including
  applicable cached bytecode, OpenSSL configuration/providers and exact argv/env.
  A label or a partial hash map cannot substitute for complete freeze validation.
- Independently verified immutable public preread, with exact canonical byte
  hashes and rechecked materialized source copies before execution.
- Independent PID/start identity binding and complete wait/reap coverage for every
  material process. Linux `wait4` bounds the maximum individual process in a
  completely reaped descendant tree; it does not measure aggregate concurrent RSS.
  This interpretation follows the [Linux resource-usage manual](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/getrusage.2.html)
  and requires an intact wait chain; detached descendants remain unknown.

  including outer observer lifetime, final evidence bytes and shared work.
- Fresh generated source and terminal identities bound to the frozen counter
  domain, archive prefix and outer ordinal. Full-size lossless measurements and
  a future exact native/HTTP host-ledger integration remain unqualified.

A first failed executed scope must close; retry, resume, substitution and
